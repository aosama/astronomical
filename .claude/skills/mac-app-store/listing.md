## Description

Your own AI, running entirely on your Mac.

Astronomical turns your Mac into a private AI studio. Pick a model, download it once, and then chat, write, create pictures and listen to replies, with nothing you type ever leaving your computer. No account. No subscription. No cloud.

ASK, WRITE, UNDERSTAND
• Get answers, drafts and clear explanations
• Drop in a photo and ask what is in it
• Attach a document and get the summary

CREATE
• Turn a sentence into a picture
• Have any reply read aloud in a natural voice

A MODEL FOR EVERY MAC
Browse a curated catalog of open models. Each one is described in plain words, with its size and how well it will run on your Mac. One click to download, and you can switch models whenever you like.

BUILT FOR DEVELOPERS TOO
A local, OpenAI-compatible API lets your coding agent or your own tools use the model on your Mac in place of a cloud service.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your prompts, files and pictures stay on your Mac. No analytics. No ads. The only thing Astronomical downloads is the models you choose.

Free and open source.

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. Models need from 1 GB to more than 20 GB of disk space each.
