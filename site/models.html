Open a model in Astronomical

These are the models Astronomical has checked. Each one downloads once, then runs entirely on your Mac. Press Open in Astronomical beside a model and the app opens on that model, with a button to download it.

You need Astronomical 0.2.105 or later on an Apple silicon Mac for the buttons to work. If nothing happens when you press one, download Astronomical for Mac first.

Good to know

  • Nothing downloads from this page. A button only opens the app. You confirm the download there.
  • Sizes are the space a model takes on disk. The app tells you how well each one runs within the memory you allow.
  • Each model has its own license, shown on its Hugging Face page. It must allow what you intend to do.
